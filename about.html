Our Journey

A Decade of Excellence

Founded in 2015 by Mr. Kaushal K. Khandelwal, Faith Automation Works has grown from a small tool room to a premier automation solutions provider. Our journey has been defined by a relentless pursuit of quality and innovation.

Located in Gurgaon's industrial corridor, we serve Tier-1 automotive manufacturers across India. Our state-of-the-art facility houses advanced machining centers and a skilled team dedicated to delivering excellence.

With over 85 machines delivered and 30+ satisfied clients, we continue to push the boundaries of automation technology. Our commitment to zero-defect engineering and customer satisfaction remains unwavering.
